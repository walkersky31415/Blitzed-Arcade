❓ HOW TO PLAY

Move: ◀ ▶ buttons, A / D or arrow keys, drag on the screen, tilt your phone (Settings), or a gamepad stick.

Boost: hold Space or the BOOST button. It slows rocks and adds score, but drains the ⚡ bar.

Ability: press E, Shift or the ⚡ button. Every skin has its own power with a cooldown.

Pause: Esc or P.

Pickups: 🪙 coins · ⏳ slows rocks · ✨ doubles score · 🛡️ blocks one hit.

Rocks: grey = normal · orange = fast · purple = splits into two · red = fired by a mini-boss. Watch for walls of rocks with one gap.

Install: on a phone use your browser menu and choose "Add to Home Screen".

Goal: earn coins, buy skins and upgrades, beat the bosses, finish daily missions, then prestige for permanent stars.
